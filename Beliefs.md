# Beliefs

## The constitution...

- **Words.** Ought to be fewer than 10,000 words
- **Veganism.** Ought to protect sentient life, as far as possible and
  practicable, from human harm.
- **Land distribution.** Ought to guarantee an equal share of land to all
  citizens.
- **Public land.** Ought to be acquired only through draws or voluntary
  direction, never by coercion.
- **Money.** Ought to be an optional medium of exchange, not a requirement for
  accessing land or resources.
- **Democracy.** Ought to avoid the ills of representative democracy, and the
  tyranny of the majority.
- **Enfranchisement.** Ought to give everybody 16 and over a chance to
  participate in the democratic process.
- **Peace.** Ought to encourage peaceable relations between citizens, and not be
  the cause of disturbance of the peace itself.
- **Drugs.** Ought to be a right to use, distribute, and cultivate, treating
  addiction as a health issue.
- **Justice.** Ought to be restorative rather than punitive.
- **Privacy.** Ought to protect individuals' information, communication,
  affairs, from unnecessary & unwarranted intrusion.
- **Distortion.** Ought to not cause unfavourable economic distortions.
- **Capitalism.** Ought to not enable capitalism to dominate, by disallowing
  absentee property ownership.
- **Private domination.** Ought to organically prevent situations where
  individuals or groups can exert disproportionate, unilateral control over
  others.
- **Barter.** Ought to allow barter, benefits-in-kind, untaxed and free.
- **Tenancy.** Ought to be rare and therefore unlegislated.
- **Family.** Ought to be unknown to the state, such that you can freely
  associate without interference, registration, or recognition.
- **Public remuneration.** Ought to, in its governments, do its accounting and
  remuneration in labour hours rather than monetary value, encouraging society
  to operate similarly.
- **Roaming.** Ought to give the right to roam.
- **Extraction.** Ought to limit and licence the removal of nonrenewable natural
  resources.
- **Separated domains.** Ought to have distinct and non-overlapping spheres of
  authority, ensuring that no single entity can dominate multiple domains of
  governance, economy, or social life. Nearlaw and Farlaw are just one example.
- **Implied right of access.** Ought to allow e.g. a postman to access private
  land, but that the title holder may withdraw implied right of access at any
  time.

## Observations

- People have incentive to inflate harm caused to them if restitution increases
  with the magnitude of the harm they report.
- People who have done harm to others may have otherwise made amends if the
  state did not prosecute them for admitting to that harm.
- If prosecution were up solely to those who had harm done to them, the
  aggressor may coerce them to refrain from seeking justice.
