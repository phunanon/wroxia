# Instruments

Instruments are part of this constitution and protected like its articles. They
hold the workings of voting and selection, so that the articles stay short. An
article uses an instrument by naming it and giving a value for each of its
parameters, which are written in _italics_.

## Instrument 1. Sticky sortition

_Draft: no article uses this instrument yet._

**Parameters:** _maximum service_, _round interval_, _reconfirmed fraction_.

1. Seats are filled by lot from volunteering citizens.
2. Every _round interval_, the _reconfirmed fraction_ of members who have gone
   longest without reconfirmation, counting from when they joined, face it.
3. Each citizen may score each member facing reconfirmation from 0 to 5. A
   member averaging 2.5 or more keeps their seat.
4. A member leaves on falling below 2.5, on reaching _maximum service_ in total,
   or on resigning or dying.
5. Empty seats are filled by lot, first from volunteers who did not just leave
   by reconfirmation, then from those who did, highest score first, with ties
   settled by lot. No one is seated beyond _maximum service_.

Example use: “chosen by Instrument 1 (_maximum service_ 25 years, _round
interval_ 2 years, _reconfirmed fraction_ one third)”.
