Wroxia land simulator

A Monte Carlo model of Part V and the land charge (V.8): monthly draws on a 48 by 48 grid of cells, nine districts, and people of five types who are born, age, inherit, move, migrate, and die. Money is in labour hours. Each line shows the mean over the runs, with a band from the lowest to the highest run.

What the model leaves out

Each person registers alone, for up to three lots; joint registration and pools of unrelated people are left out, and only families and savvy parents admit children to pool. Each cell is a piece of vacant land, so anyone may register for any adjacent cells as one. Charges are frozen in hours at taking, split pro rata by area on notice for part. Starter plots are the least desirable vacant cells and are not open to ordinary draws. Arrears are recovered at once from the building sum. Directions are made at death, to a child, or by one in ten sole holders who leave, or half if cheaper than a draw, to a landless adult child or else a landless seeker.
